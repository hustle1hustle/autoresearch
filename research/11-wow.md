# Stage 2 — Wow scoring (feasibility-blind)

Each template scored 1–5 on the rubric: **E**ffortless (login UX), **R**ecognition, **T**ier/percentile payload, **M**agic, **P**ayoff (3-part). **/25.** Feasibility-blind — a flex is scored at its *aspirational best* (e.g. Spotify "Top 0.1%" is scored as the flex it is; whether the percentile is API-reachable is the Stage 4 gate). **Raw counts are zeroed on T** per principle #3. Effortless here is a *login-UX estimate*; Stage 3 refines it with the v1 auth matrix.

> Read E as a preview of login friction: Spotify/Farcaster/GitHub already score high on E; Binance/airline/prop-firm already drag. Stage 3 amplifies that; Stage 4 then kills the infeasible.

## The emotional trigger + 3-part payoff (top 5)
1. **Spotify Top 0.1%** — *"it knows my soul."* Wrapped is already a screenshot ritual. Payoff: **Music Power Users** community + per-artist **presale/early-ticket** offer + a Wrapped-style **story card**. The cultural hero.
2. **Riot Radiant** — *"I'm objectively elite."* Rank IS identity for this cohort. Payoff: **per-game elite gate** + tournaments/coaching/gear + **rank leaderboard**.
3. **Binance Verified PnL** — *"receipts, not LARP."* In a base full of unverifiable PnL screenshots, a *cryptographically verified* one is the ultimate flex. Payoff: **Top Traders** gate + **fee rebates / alpha** + PnL **leaderboard**. (Resonates hardest with the 3.5M base.)
4. **Whoop "Whoop Age"** — *"my body is younger than yours."* Deeply personal, biometric, unfakeable. Payoff: **biohacker** club + device perks + age-gap **story**.
5. **Airline Diamond / 48 countries** — *"I live in the front cabin."* Pure status. Payoff: **Frequent Travelers** + **−22% hotel / upgrades** + countries **story**. *Caveat: only if Stage 3 finds a non-password login.*

## What Stage 3 will do to this list
Re-rank by **wow × login-effortlessness**. Stage 4 then gates feasibility (Spotify percentile reachability, Binance attestation/WAF, airline no-API, Riot API-gating).
